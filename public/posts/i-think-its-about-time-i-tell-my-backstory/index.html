I think its about time I tell you guys my backstory

Aug 2, 2025

Imported from LinkedIn and X (Twitter)

For those who don't know (and I'm sure many of you don't), I'm actually 19 years old. I decided to mention this finally as I've seen an influx of young people getting into tech, one that actually sent me a connection request and I accepted it.

I always have wanted to own my own business, even when I was real young. The reason is not just about money either. I wanted to make money doing something I enjoy doing, and still continue to want to. I could just go out and work a full time job all my life that I hate doing, but thats not how I want to spend all my life.

I'm still in the process of getting my business off the gound, and if you know of anyone wanting any of my services, please let me know. Anyway, my first business idea was not in tech, It was in the restaurant industry. I wanted to start my own restaurant selling the burgers and such.

I know it says on my LinkedIn page I live in New Orleans, but I'm not originally from nola. I'm originally from another city in Alabama called Montgomery.

I just wanted to go ahead and mention that as this idea had nothing to do with nola food, it was simple American food like burgers and fries. I realized (not super quick though) that I didnt want to go that path, and tbh I'm glad I didn't go that route because of you know why (covid) and also less obvious reasons like I realised I don't like cooking enough.

With that being said the next idea was still not tech based, I had ideas on starting a company where I sold light globes and other electrical stuff as I know how do electrical work, though I'm not an electrician just so you know, but I realised that was not realistic and thats when I finally started getting into tech.

I started learning how to code at 12 years old, and I started my first site named after myself sewellstephens[.]com after registering that domain with some gift cards I got as Christmas gifts as mind you I was only 12. I still run the site to this day, but now it just shows some basic info about myself. Back then tough, I kept changing what the site was about so many times I can't remember exactly it all, but I ended up making it into a site where I had this software I was trying to sell to try to automate marketing for businesses. This was 2018 mind you so no ChatGPT yet. I never could get it to work and finally gave up two years later during covid at 14 years old.

I went onto my next project which was on a completely different site domain rototer[.]com. It promised to help companies with compliance and GDPR by offering a cookie banner which actually wasn't compliant in the end and a very simple privacy policy and terms and conditions generator that worked with some basic javascript that filled in the details of an existing privacy policy template I essentially took from another tool that did that did the same thing which doesn't exist anymore either.

So then at 15 I created my next product twayobiz[.]com. Its no longer online either, but it essentially was a file sharing service I created that I actually did get signups on, but ended up shutting it down as people started abusing it heavily and didn't have the resources to do much.

I shut it down, but not until a year after I created my next product obeatow[.]com. This product aimed to provide a customer feedback software like none other, offering roadmap feature along with a customer support widget like non other you could integrate with your existing live chat service. I shut this one down too.

I then created what was going to be an AI coding assistant called coadfiller[.]biz (think cursor), but shut it down within two months after launching it in jan 2023 at 17 years old. I wouldn't finally turn 18 until later that year, but after that I created an AI image generation software called imgartfiscreator[.]club. I was running out of business names, so I just came up with something super random but was gone within six months.

After that after turning 18, I didn't really have a software product for a little while aside from Obeatow as I hadn't shut that one down quite yet, but I wasn't very satisfied with anything I had created so far.

I took a different approach this time for my next SaaS krastie[.]ai (current one) and created my SaaS app in React, as I was using low code for basically everything and built out all my SaaS UI's in Wix all those years.

I still built my landing page in a page builder, but used Webflow this time as I saw noticed a lot of companies (primarly SaaS companies) used it for their landing pages (and still do) as I heard its best to host your landing page and web app separately after doing some reasearch (which explained to me why app. is used in front of web app urls).

That is everything now...
